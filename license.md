## ❌ The Problem: Email Communication Is Not Private

Sending emails exposes your identity and personal information to multiple parties:

| **Problem** | **Why It's a Privacy Risk** |
| :--- | :--- |
| **IP Address Exposure** | Every email reveals your IP address. |
| **Email Headers** | Headers contain routing information and device details. |
| **Metadata Tracking** | Servers log when, where, and how you send emails. |
| **Third-Party Access** | Email providers can access and share your data. |
| **Tracking Links** | Senders can track when emails are opened. |
| **Government Requests** | Providers may be compelled to reveal user identities. |

> **🔥 The Result?** Your privacy is compromised every time you send an email.
